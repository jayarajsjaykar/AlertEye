    The Problem

    Cameras that only record can't stop what they see.

    Conventional CCTV relies on human operators watching dozens of screens at once. That means delayed responses, missed incidents, higher staffing costs, and risks that were preventable — if anyone had been looking at the right screen at the right second.

    Traditional CCTV

    • Footage is reviewed after an incident, not during it
    • One operator can realistically watch a handful of feeds
    • Threats are caught by chance, not by design
    • Evidence exists, but the response was already too late

    AlertEye

    • Every feed is analyzed continuously, by AI, in real time
    • Suspicious activity triggers an instant alert to the right team
    • Incidents are flagged as they begin, not after they end
    • Security teams act on intelligence, not just footage
    Core Capabilities

    One platform, watching for everything that matters.

    AlertEye ships with a full library of detection models — tuned for security, safety, and compliance — running on the cameras you already have.

    Real-time AI monitoring

    Every camera stream analyzed continuously, not sampled or reviewed after the fact.

    Intrusion detection

    Flags entry into restricted zones and perimeters the moment it happens.

    Violence & fight detection

    Recognizes physical altercations in progress and escalates instantly.

    Fire & smoke detection

    Catches early visual signs of fire before smoke alarms would ever trigger.

    Weapon detection

    Identifies visible firearms and edged weapons in the frame in real time.

    Suspicious behavior

    Learns typical patterns of movement and flags what breaks from them.

    Abandoned object

    Notices bags or packages left unattended in monitored areas.

    Unauthorized access

    Alerts when someone enters a secured door, gate, or zone without clearance.

    Face recognition

    Identity matching for access and watchlists, deployed where legally permitted.

    Vehicle detection & ANPR

    Tracks vehicles and reads plates automatically at gates and lots.

    PPE compliance

    Confirms helmets, vests, and safety gear are worn on site.

    Cross-camera tracking

    Follows a person or vehicle's path across every connected camera.
